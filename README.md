# Welcome to ErkeScan

ErkeScan brings market screening, strategy signals and trading tools into one web app. Start with the task you want to do:

| Your task | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Find active futures markets and build a watchlist | [Screener](https://app.erkescan.com/screener) |
| Get a Telegram message when your own conditions match | [Custom alerts](alerts/how-alerts-work.md) |
| Follow published strategies or investigate a market catalyst | [Signals and Radar](signals/choose-a-tool.md) |
| Connect automatic execution of Vertex signals on your Bybit account | [Copy trading](copy-trading/overview.md) |
| Review your trades | [Trading Journal](journal/overview.md) |
| Compare market activity or explore options positioning | [Charts](https://app.erkescan.com/charts) and [GEX](gex/overview.md) |

The screener, custom alerts and Charts cover Binance USDT perpetual futures. The other sections have their own markets, connection requirements and access rules. A signal notification does not place an order. Copy trading is a separate feature that requires an exchange connection, risk settings and explicit activation.

## New here?

1. [Create your account](getting-started/create-your-account.md).
2. [Choose a plan](getting-started/choose-a-plan.md). Compare the term and total price before paying.
3. [Connect Telegram](getting-started/connect-integrations.md) if you want messages. You do not need to buy another subscription to connect it.
4. [Spend your first 15 minutes in the screener](getting-started/first-steps.md), or open the product you came for from the table above.

There is no public free trial on the standard pricing page. You can read the public strategy pages before buying. Premium live signal details and the paid screener require active access. A subscription does not start copy trading or connect an exchange account automatically.

## Before and after subscribing

Strategy records are public. Active paid access opens premium live details where offered. A page can legitimately have no current signal: that is different from a locked card or an error loading data.

On a strategy signal, read the symbol, direction, entry, stop, targets, time and status shown by that strategy. Do not apply one strategy's stop distance or trade duration to another. Radar has a different purpose: its spotted price, catalyst and observed movement do not form an entry/stop/target trade.

## Read the record correctly

Check the displayed period and methodology before comparing results. Profitable trades, profit factor and trade count describe the strategy record; they do not show what your own exchange account earned. Fees, funding, fills, timing and your chosen risk can change the result of execution.

Radar's **Worked** and **Peak 48h** measures concern subsequent price movement. A peak is not an executed exit or realized profit. A young observation may still be developing.

If data could not be refreshed, do not assume the last displayed number is current. Check the page's state and time; ask support when something appears stuck. Past results do not guarantee future returns.

## Receive notifications in Telegram

1. Open the tool's page and find its Telegram control.
2. If your chat is not linked, use the personal connection link and press **Start** in Telegram.
3. Return to the page and check that delivery is enabled for that tool.
4. Repeat the delivery choice for any other strategy you want.

Messages arrive through [@erkescanalert_bot](https://t.me/erkescanalert_bot). Each strategy/Radar control is separate from custom alerts. Switching one off does not pause a trading bot. [Connection help](../getting-started/connect-integrations.md).

## Signals are not automatic execution

Receiving Vertex or Helix notifications does not connect your exchange. Automatic execution lives in [Copy trading](../copy-trading/overview.md) and requires its own setup and activation. Vertex copy trading is available; Helix copy trading is marked as preparing even though Helix Signals has a live signal page.

## Community access

Discord/community access is a separate membership route and is not established by clicking a signal's Telegram button. Check [Discord](../integrations/discord.md) or contact [support](../reference/support.md) if your access comes through a community. Do not assume buying a standard plan enrols you in a course or grants a Discord role.

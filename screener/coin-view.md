# Coin view

Use the chart above the screener for a quick price check, or open the symbol page to keep one instrument visible in a separate tab.

## Open a chart from the table

On desktop, click the **ticker text** in the Symbol cell. The chart opens over the table. The favourite control beside the ticker is separate; clicking the rest of the row does not open this chart.

The embedded TradingView chart opens on the 15-minute interval. Check its exchange, instrument and interval before comparing it with the row. A ticker label alone does not identify a market: spot, perpetual and another exchange's contract can have different prices and history. Some instruments may have incomplete or unavailable external charts.

Close the panel with **Escape**, **×**, or the dimmed background. **Full Page →** opens `/symbols/<SYMBOL>` in another tab. The chart displays price history; return to the table for ErkeScan's RVOL, VDelta, RetailHeat, OI change and funding values.

## On mobile

The narrow layout uses cards. Tap a card to open the symbol page. The star button adds or removes a favourite without opening the page.

## Symbol pages

Symbol pages require an active subscription. The app normalises supported ticker spellings and checks membership against its available symbol list. An invalid or unsupported ticker may return a 404. If the symbol-list check is temporarily unavailable, the chart can still render, so a rendered widget is not proof of a current listing.

There is no general `/symbols` directory page. Find an instrument through screener search and open its ticker. If a chart fails, verify the instrument on the exchange and return to the screener to check data freshness; do not infer a delisting from an external widget alone.

For comparing a group of instruments, use [Charts](charts.md). For a repeatable review, see [From a row to a checked observation](from-screen-to-trade.md).

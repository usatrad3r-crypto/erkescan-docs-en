# Frequently asked questions

The questions people actually ask before and just after they subscribe, answered honestly and
briefly. Each answer links to the page with the full version.

## Fastest answers

| Question | Short answer |
|---|---|
| Which market is covered? | Binance USDT-margined perpetual futures only — roughly 670 contracts, around 140 of them tokenized stocks and commodities. [Details](../reference/data-and-coverage.md) |
| What does it cost? | $200 for a month, $468 for three, $868 for a year. All three unlock exactly the same features. [Details](../getting-started/choose-a-plan.md) |
| Is there a free trial? | No. There is no trial and no free tier. |
| How many alerts can I have? | 200, on every plan. [Details](../alerts/how-alerts-work.md) |
| Where do alerts arrive? | In Telegram, via **@erkescanalert_bot**. Nothing is sent until your Telegram chat is linked to your account. [How to link it](../getting-started/connect-integrations.md) |
| How do I sort a column? | With the small caret button beside the header. The header label itself drags the column. [Details](../screener/personalize.md) |
| Does it auto-renew? | No. Every payment is one-time; you renew when you choose. |
| Who do I contact? | [@ErkeScanSupportbot](https://t.me/ErkeScanSupportbot) or support@erkescan.com. [Details](../reference/support.md) |

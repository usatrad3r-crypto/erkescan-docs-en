# Subscription and billing

## Prices and differences between plans

The standard plans differ by term and total price, with the same standard feature access. See the current table in [Choose a plan](../getting-started/choose-a-plan.md) and confirm the total at checkout. A future or restricted feature still follows its own availability and setup requirements.

## Is there a trial or lifetime plan?

Neither is offered on the standard public pricing page. A historical account arrangement or a time-limited promotion is not a public offer for everyone. Check the official purchase page for the offer available now.

## Payment and activation

Use the [pricing page](https://app.erkescan.com/pricing) or [@erkescan_premium_bot](https://t.me/erkescan_premium_bot). Payment methods are those offered at checkout. In the bot, use the same email as your ErkeScan account.

After payment, check [Subscription](https://app.erkescan.com/payments/billing). Processing or received means the payment/access flow is not yet complete. A crypto transfer can require network confirmation. If you already sent the money, keep the payment reference and contact [support](../reference/support.md) before paying again.

## Automatic renewal or cancellation

Standard ErkeScan term purchases are one-time payments. You renew manually; there is no recurring charge to cancel for those purchases. To stop buying access, do not renew. Account deletion is a separate action.

Community membership can have different billing arrangements. Use the membership-management link shown on your account where applicable rather than assuming it follows the standard term-purchase rule.

## What happens if I renew early?

The new term is added to your remaining paid time. Check the resulting expiry on Subscription. Keep a record if the date looks wrong and contact support.

## What happens at expiry?

Paid access ends; premium delivery requires renewed entitlement. Custom alerts can be paused by the system, while sessions and community access may update on the periodic access check. Do not rely on a promised extra hour after the displayed expiry.

Telegram renewal reminders depend on a linked, reachable chat. The date on your subscription page is the reference even if no reminder arrives.

For an active trading bot, see [Copy trading management](../copy-trading/manage-and-troubleshoot.md) for the distinction between admitting new trades and managing existing positions. A billing page is not evidence that a position has closed.

## What returns after renewal?

Alerts paused by the system can resume. Alerts you paused yourself stay paused. Check strategy delivery switches and use a fresh community invite if you need to rejoin a group. If access is active but a feature remains unavailable, tell support; do not purchase a second term to force it.

## Refund request

The published Terms generally exclude refunds for a change of mind or unused time, subject to the exceptions in Sections 6–7, payment-method rules and applicable mandatory consumer rights.

Contact <support@erkescan.com> with your account email, plan and payment reference. The request is assessed under the published [Terms](https://erkescan.com/terms), including their exceptions and applicable mandatory consumer rights. The bot does not approve refunds. Read the immediate-access consent shown at checkout alongside the withdrawal provisions in the Terms.

## What is a Discord plan?

Access linked to eligible community membership, rather than an ordinary purchase on the pricing page. Its continued validity depends on that membership. A missing end date is not a lifetime promise. [Discord](../integrations/discord.md).

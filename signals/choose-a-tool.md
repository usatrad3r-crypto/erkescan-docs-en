# Screener, alerts, signals, Radar or copy trading?

These tools answer different questions. Choose the one that matches what you want to do, and do not assume that opening a signal page or receiving a Telegram alert places a trade.

| Your task | Where to start | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find instruments that match market conditions | Screener | You filter and compare market data, then investigate candidates. |
| Be notified when your own conditions match | Alerts | Your saved alert rules produce notifications. |
| Read a strategy's entry, stop and target plan | [Signals](https://app.erkescan.com/signals) | You open a strategy page and inspect its current state and history. |
| Investigate a price move with recent news | Radar, when shown in Signals | You get a research lead and its news context, not a complete trade plan. |
| Let Vertex execute eligible entries on your account | [Copy trading](https://app.erkescan.com/copy-trading) | A separately connected and started bot can place and manage orders on Bybit. |
| Review your own completed trades | [Journal](https://app.erkescan.com/en/journal) | Your exchange history is imported for analysis. |

## Strategy signals and notifications

The Signals catalog is the entry point to strategy pages such as Alpha Pulse and Vertex Signals. Each strategy has its own rules and statistics. Read the plan and the page's explanation; an instrument's general market bias is not necessarily the direction of a specific strategy trade.

Alpha Pulse focuses on its weekend setup. Vertex Signals presents its own trade plans and results. Helix signals, where available, are another separate strategy. Availability of a strategy's page or Telegram notifications does not imply that its copy-trading bot is available: **Helix copy trading is still in preparation**.

To receive a strategy's Telegram notifications, use the Telegram control on that strategy page and complete the linking flow shown. Review its final enabled/disabled state. This is separate from creating your own condition-based alert and separate from starting Vertex copy trading.

No current signal is a normal state. A historical trade, a completed plan or old notification is not automatically a fresh entry opportunity. Read the signal's timestamp and state before using its levels.

## Radar is a research tool

Radar brings together a market move and relevant fresh news. Its displayed reference price is context for that observation. A Radar item does not contain the entry, stop and target structure of a strategy signal, and it does not start copy trading.

Use it to decide what to investigate: open the source, check publication time, inspect the chart and decide whether the move still makes sense. A news item and a price move occurring together do not by themselves establish cause or predict the next move.

## Market dashboards are context

Market dashboards and charts help describe current conditions. An indicator, score, label or level is not proof that an order has been placed. For execution, check the Copy trading account and Bybit; for your personal result, check exchange history and the Journal.

Statistics on different screens may use different definitions. Compare the same period and methodology. In particular, the Journal defines 1R as 1% of equity at entry; do not automatically equate it with a signal strategy's stop-based R or a Vertex risk percentage.

# Discord

Discord linking is for customers whose ErkeScan access comes through an eligible existing community membership. It is optional for a normal paid ErkeScan account. You do not need Discord to use the screener or connect Telegram delivery.

Connecting an identity, joining a server and receiving a membership role are three different actions. The ErkeScan connection identifies your Discord account and checks membership; it does not itself enrol you in a course, join a server or grant a role.

## Connect from ErkeScan

1. Sign in to the ErkeScan account you want to use.
2. Open [Integrations](https://app.erkescan.com/integrations) from the avatar menu.
3. Select the Discord connect button.
4. Authorize on Discord using the account with your membership.
5. Return to Integrations and read the result.

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Disconnected | No Discord identity is connected |
| Linked | The identity is connected; eligible active membership has not been confirmed |
| Connected | The identity and active membership are recognized |

If you see Linked, check that you used the right Discord account and that its membership is active. Do not pay again simply to fix an identity mismatch. Contact [support](../reference/support.md) with your ErkeScan email and the state shown.

## Connect through the subscription bot

In [@erkescan_premium_bot](https://t.me/erkescan_premium_bot), choose **Connect your Discord**, enter the same email as your ErkeScan account, follow the authorization link and return to Telegram to press **Confirm** before it expires. This route can also establish your Telegram chat connection.

The bot may provide a community invitation where your access allows it. Use the current invitation it gives you; a restored entitlement does not automatically rejoin a group you have left.

## How long access lasts

Community access depends on active membership and is checked periodically. A Discord plan with no ErkeScan end date is not a lifetime guarantee. The billing page can display membership renewal information and a separate management link where available; follow those terms for community billing.

Standard ErkeScan purchases and community membership are separate routes. Do not assume the “no automatic renewal” rule for a website term purchase applies to a third-party community membership.

If membership is removed, access based on it can end. On renewal, system-paused custom alerts may resume, but alerts you paused yourself stay paused.

## Disconnect or fix an error

Use **Disconnect Discord** and confirm only when you mean to remove the identity. If your ErkeScan access depends on that membership, disconnecting can remove that access. Switching to a different Discord account can also change the entitlement.

For an expired or incomplete authorization, start again from Integrations. For too many attempts, wait before retrying. For a Discord outage, wait and retry; the app cannot confirm membership while the provider is unavailable. If a paid account still loses access, contact support instead of repeatedly disconnecting it.

Discord linking does not control the separate Copy trading bot or the delivery switches on strategy pages.

# 1. Create your account

1. Open [Sign up](https://app.erkescan.com/sign-up).
2. Use one of the sign-up methods offered in the form and complete any verification it requests.
3. After signing in, open [Subscription](https://app.erkescan.com/payments/billing) from the avatar menu and check the account email.

Use this same email if you buy through the Telegram subscription bot. A different email can leave the payment and the account you are viewing separate. If you already paid, check the email and contact support before buying again.

Creating an account does not itself activate a paid subscription or start copy trading.

## Returning later

Open [Sign in](https://app.erkescan.com/sign-in) and use the same account identity. Follow the verification or recovery steps shown in that form. Do not send a password, verification code or sign-in link to support or another person.

## What can I see before paying?

| Page | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| [Pricing](https://app.erkescan.com/pricing) | Public plans and prices |
| [Signals](https://app.erkescan.com/signals) | Public catalogue and strategy records; premium live details require access |
| Screener, Alerts, Charts | Paid data and tools require an active subscription; otherwise an upgrade panel appears |
| [Subscription](https://app.erkescan.com/payments/billing) | Account status and purchase information after signing in |
| [Integrations](https://app.erkescan.com/integrations) | Your Telegram and Discord connection controls after signing in |

There is no public trial on the standard pricing page. Account access and a connected exchange are different things: the Journal and copy trading have separate setup flows.

## Find your way around

The top menu includes the screener, signals and charts. Depending on your access, it also shows Copy trading, Journal and GEX. Use the product page to check what is available to your account. On a phone, open the navigation menu.

The avatar menu contains **Subscription**, **Alerts** and **Integrations**. **Contact us** opens the support bot.

The **EN | RU** control changes language. Most pages keep the same address. The Journal uses language paths, including `/en/journal` and `/ru/journal`. If an authentication window retains its previous language, reload it.

## Account changes or closure

Use the account controls for changes available there. For closure, payment attribution or privacy requests, contact <support@erkescan.com>. Account closure and letting a paid term expire are different actions; review the [Terms](https://erkescan.com/terms) and [Privacy Policy](https://erkescan.com/privacy-policy) before deleting an account. Do not assume that deletion refunds a purchase or removes financial records immediately.

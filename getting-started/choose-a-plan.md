# 2. Choose a plan

Open [app.erkescan.com/pricing](https://app.erkescan.com/pricing). These are the standard public prices checked on **26 September 2026**:

| Plan | You pay for the whole term | Average per month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly — 1 month | **$200** | $200 |
| Quarterly — 3 months | **$468** | $156 |
| Annual — 12 months | **$868** | About $72.33 |

The plans have the same standard feature access and differ by term and price. The annual figure is an average, not a monthly instalment. The total, any applicable taxes and the available payment methods are shown before you pay. Use the checkout price for a new purchase or renewal.

The standard page has no public free trial or lifetime offer. A separate promotion applies only when its official page shows an active offer; an old promotional link is not evidence that the price is still available.

## What you are buying

Active access unlocks paid screener data, custom alerts, charts and premium signal details. Telegram delivery still needs a linked chat and the appropriate strategy switch. Product pages explain any additional requirements. Buying a subscription does not connect an exchange, activate a bot, or promise access to a feature marked as preparing.

Discord/community membership is a separate access route. Do not buy a standard plan on the assumption that it enrols you in a course or automatically grants a Discord role. [Discord details](../integrations/discord.md).

## Pay on the website

1. Sign in and select a plan on the pricing page.
2. Confirm the account, plan, total and terms on the checkout page.
3. Choose an offered payment method. Cryptocurrency checkout may display **CryptoCloud / TryBit** branding. Card payment is available only when its button is offered.
4. For crypto, use the exact asset, network, amount and address from the current invoice. Observe the expiry time shown there; do not send to an old invoice.
5. Return to ErkeScan and check [Subscription](https://app.erkescan.com/payments/billing).

Keep your payment reference. Network confirmation and activation can take time. If the payment is already sent but access is still pending, do not pay again: check the subscription status and contact [support](../reference/support.md) with the reference, payment time and account email.

## Pay through Telegram

Open [@erkescan_premium_bot](https://t.me/erkescan_premium_bot), send `/start`, choose a language, then **Subscribe** and a term. Enter the **same email as your ErkeScan account** and follow the bot's payment link. This route uses crypto and can also link your Telegram chat. Check delivery afterwards through [Integrations](https://app.erkescan.com/integrations).

## Renewal and expiry

Standard ErkeScan term purchases are **one-time payments with manual renewal**. There is no automatic repeat charge for these purchases. An early renewal adds the term to your remaining paid time; confirm the resulting end date on Subscription.

When the term ends, paid access ends. Custom alerts can be paused by the system and premium delivery requires renewed access. Community permissions and sessions may update during the periodic access check. A linked, reachable Telegram chat can receive renewal reminders; keep track of your end date yourself as well.

After renewal, alerts paused by the system can resume. Alerts you paused yourself stay paused. Check your alert list and strategy toggles if delivery does not return. Rejoining a Telegram group may require using a fresh invite; restored eligibility is not the same as being back inside the group.

Access granted through a community follows that membership's own rules. A Discord access label is not a promise of lifetime access, and its renewal arrangements may differ from the standard purchases above.
